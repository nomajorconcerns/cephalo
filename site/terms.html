What Cephalo is, and what it is not

Cephalo checks the commands and file accesses that supported agents send to its hooks against rules, and blocks the ones that match. It is a seatbelt, not a sandbox. It does not see inside script files, cannot catch commands built at run time, and can be worked around by anything that runs as you outside its hooks. A rule can miss something or block something harmless. Read the "Honest limits" section of the main page. You stay responsible for what your agents do and for keeping backups.
