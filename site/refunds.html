Refunds

Last updated November 5, 2026

If you buy Pro or Team and it is not right for you, you can have your first payment back within 14 days.

The 14-day refund

  • Email zuri77290@gmail.com within 14 days of your first payment and ask for a refund. You get the full amount of that first payment back. You do not need to give a reason.
  • Include the email address you bought with, and the order number from your Lemon Squeezy receipt if you have it. Please do not send your license key in the email; the order number is enough.
  • Refunds go back to the original payment method through Lemon Squeezy, which is the seller of record. Your bank decides how long it takes to show up.
  • The 14 days start on the date of your first payment. If you started with a discount (for example CEPHALOG), you are refunded what you actually paid.

After a refund

A refunded license is disabled, so the paid features stop working. The Pro package checks its license about every 35 days (cephalo pro renew), so there can be a short gap before it stops. Everything in the free software keeps working exactly as before, because it never needed a license. You can run cephalo pro deactivate to give the seat back.

Cancelling

You can cancel a subscription at any time. It stays active until the end of the period you paid for, and then it does not renew. A cancellation by itself does not refund the current period. If you cancel inside the 14 days, ask for a refund as above.

Renewals and anything else

The 14-day refund covers the first payment. If a renewal charged you and you did not mean to keep the plan, or something else went wrong with a charge, email zuri77290@gmail.com and we will look at it.

The free software

Cephalo version 0.1.1 and everything that was in it is free, so there is nothing to refund.
