Refunds

Last updated November 16, 2026

Cephalo itself is free, so there is nothing to refund. Skills bought on Agensi (the Cephalo agent guard skill and SentryKit) are refunded by Agensi under its own policy.

Skills bought on Agensi

The Cephalo agent guard skill and the SentryKit skills are sold on the Agensi marketplace, not through this site. Agensi takes the payment, and refunds for those purchases follow Agensi's refund policy (section 5.5, "Money-Back Guarantee", of Agensi's Terms of Service). Ask Agensi for a refund as that policy describes. A skill purchase is a skill download only: it does not include a Cephalo license key or any paid Cephalo feature, so there is nothing on this site to refund or disable.
